Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

LightBluePrep is a free, non-commercial SAT practice site run by a student. You can use all of it without an account.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your answers, notes, highlights, marks and practice sets are saved only in your own browser's storage on your device.

Who your browser talks to

  • Cloudflare serves the website itself, and like any web host it handles your IP address to deliver the page.
  • College Board supplies the questions. Your browser downloads them directly from College Board; we never host or store question content.
  • Desmos supplies the calculator, and only if you open it.

Accounts and sync

Signing in is not available yet. If it is added, this page will be updated before it goes live, and signing in will stay optional.

Deleting your data

Your data lives in your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings deletes all of it.

Children

LightBluePrep is meant for high school students preparing for the SAT. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on the GitHub repository, where all of the site's code is public.
